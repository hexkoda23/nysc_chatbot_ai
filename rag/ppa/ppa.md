# Place of Primary Assignment

A Place of Primary Assignment, or PPA, is the organization where a corps member performs the main service after orientation camp. A PPA can be a school, government office, health facility, approved private organization, or another employer accepted by NYSC.

Report to your PPA with your posting letter. If the PPA accepts you, complete the acceptance process and submit required documents to NYSC through your Local Government Inspector or the state procedure.

## Rejection and Change of PPA

If your PPA rejects you, collect a rejection letter and report to your Local Government Inspector or NYSC office for reposting. Do not stay at home without reporting.

You should not reject a PPA casually. If there is a serious reason, such as unsafe conditions, health risk, or a genuine mismatch with NYSC posting rules, report through your LGI and request guidance. Final approval for PPA changes comes from NYSC, not only the employer.

Serving in a private company may be allowed if NYSC approves the posting. Serving in your own company or arranging a fake PPA is risky and should not be done without clear NYSC approval.

## Attendance and Allowance

Failure to report to your PPA can affect clearance and allowance. Your PPA may pay extra allowance if it chooses, but that does not replace federal NYSC allowance. PPA payments are separate from NYSC allowance and depend on the employer.

If your PPA is unsafe, document the concern, inform your LGI quickly, and avoid taking unofficial shortcuts.
