# Skills Acquisition and Entrepreneurship Development

SAED means Skills Acquisition and Entrepreneurship Development. It exposes corps members to practical skills and business ideas during the service year, especially during orientation camp and post-camp training.

Corps members may attend sensitization, choose skill areas, join training sessions, and continue with post-camp skill development where available. SAED participation can help with self-employment, but it does not replace PPA, CDS, or clearance obligations.

Follow camp and state SAED instructions for available skill groups, schedules, and approved trainers.
