# NYSC Orientation Camp

NYSC orientation camp normally lasts about 21 days. During camp, corps members complete physical registration, receive kits, join platoons, attend lectures and drills, complete SAED activities, and prepare for posting to a Place of Primary Assignment.

## Documents to Take to Camp

Bring your call-up letter, green card, original statement of result or certificate, school ID card, passport photographs, medical fitness certificate from a government or military hospital, and any extra professional or foreign-trained graduate documents that apply to you. Carry photocopies because camp officials may keep copies.

## Useful Items

Useful items include white round-neck shirts, white shorts, white socks, white canvas, toiletries, simple bedding, padlocks, torchlight or rechargeable lamp, basic medicine prescribed for you, and enough cash or card access for personal expenses. Keep valuables safe.

## Prohibited or Risky Items

Avoid prohibited items such as weapons, illegal drugs, fake documents, military camouflage not issued by NYSC, and electrical appliances or items camp officials disallow. Follow the instructions on your call-up letter and from camp officials.

## Medical and Family Situations

A medical certificate of fitness is required for camp registration. Pregnant women and nursing mothers should follow NYSC instructions for their category and confirm arrangements before travelling. Local guidance states that expectant and nursing mothers may not be accommodated in camp.

You can use your phone in camp, but obey camp rules on when and where phones may be used. Do not record restricted activities or ignore instructions from camp officials.

Leaving camp before the end of orientation requires official permission. Do not leave camp without approval.
