# Exemption and Exclusion

An NYSC exemption certificate is issued to graduates who are not required to serve because they meet exemption conditions, such as being above the service age at graduation or other lawful grounds. Confirm your own status from NYSC or your institution because eligibility depends on official records.

Exclusion is different from exemption. Exclusion is commonly used for categories such as part-time, distance learning, or other programmes that are not mobilized for regular national service. Exempted graduates receive exemption certificates, while excluded graduates receive exclusion letters.

Graduates above 30 at the time of graduation are usually processed for exemption. Someone who is above 30 at registration may still serve if they graduated before turning 30 and NYSC records confirm eligibility.

Part-time graduates are generally processed for exclusion, not regular service. Foreign-trained graduates can be eligible for service or exemption depending on age, qualification, and NYSC verification.

## Certificates

Collection of exemption or exclusion documents follows NYSC and institutional procedures. Locally trained graduates may receive documents through their institution or NYSC channels. Foreign-trained graduates should follow NYSC portal and verification instructions.

If an exemption certificate has an error, report it through your institution or NYSC official channels. Do not alter the certificate yourself.

NYSC discharge, exemption, or exclusion documents are important for many employment and public service processes in Nigeria. Employers may ask for them where applicable.

Certificate verification and replacement of lost certificates should be done through official NYSC channels. Do not use third-party agents who promise shortcuts.
