# NYSC Registration and Mobilization

NYSC online registration is done on the NYSC portal after your institution has uploaded and cleared your details for mobilization. You normally need your National Identification Number, JAMB registration number, matriculation number, a working email address, phone number, passport photograph, scanned signature, and accurate biodata.

Your name must appear on the approved Senate or Academic Board list before you can complete mobilization. If your name is not on the Senate list, contact your institution's Student Affairs or NYSC mobilization office. NYSC cannot register you if your school has not uploaded or corrected your details.

The graduation list is the broad school list of graduates submitted for mobilization. The Senate list is the approved list used by NYSC to confirm that a graduate is eligible to register. A name can be on a school graduation list but still require correction or approval before appearing properly for NYSC registration.

## Online Registration Steps

1. Visit the NYSC portal.
2. Choose fresh registration if you are registering for the first time.
3. Use revalidation only if you were deployed before but did not report to camp.
4. Fill your biodata carefully.
5. Complete biometric capture yourself. Do not use a proxy.
6. Upload required documents for your category.
7. Preview all details before submitting.
8. Print your green card after successful registration.
9. Return to the dashboard when deployment is released to print your call-up letter.

## Corrections

Name spelling, name arrangement, course, date of birth, and graduation date issues should be treated carefully. Some corrections start from your school, while some are requested on the NYSC portal. Do not create another portal account to fix an error.

For date of birth correction, follow the option shown on your NYSC dashboard if available. If the issue came from your school record, report it to your institution quickly. For name arrangement or spelling issues, use the correction option on the portal where available and involve your school if the portal asks for institutional correction.

## Age and Exemption

Graduates who were above 30 years old at the time of graduation are usually processed for exemption instead of regular service. The relevant date is normally the age at graduation, not the age at online registration. Confirm your specific case with NYSC or your institution.

## Foreign-trained Graduates

Foreign-trained graduates can register for NYSC if they meet NYSC requirements. They should prepare original degree certificates, international passport or travel documents, evidence of admission and graduation, transcript or confirmation where required, and any Federal Ministry of Education evaluation or verification documents requested by NYSC. They may also need physical verification before call-up.

Do not upload fake, edited, or downloaded certificates. Fraudulent documents can lead to decampment, cancellation of service, and referral to law enforcement.
