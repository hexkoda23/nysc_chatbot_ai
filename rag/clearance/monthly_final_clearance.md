# NYSC Clearance

Monthly clearance is the NYSC process used to confirm that a corps member is present, serving, and eligible for monthly allowance. It may include biometric verification, PPA confirmation, CDS attendance, and LGI checks.

Biometric clearance is compulsory where NYSC requires it. If biometric clearance fails, report immediately to your LGI or the clearance officer. Do not leave without making sure the failed attempt is recorded and escalated.

Missing monthly clearance can affect allowance and service record. If you miss clearance for medical or other serious reasons, keep evidence and report through official channels as soon as possible.

Clearance is normally done in your state or local government of service. Do not assume you can clear from another state unless NYSC gives permission.

## Final Clearance

Final clearance is the end-of-service process before passing out. Documents can include final clearance forms, PPA clearance letter, CDS records, identity documents, and any state-specific NYSC requirements. Follow instructions from your LGI and state secretariat.

If your PPA refuses to sign clearance, find out the reason, resolve genuine attendance or work issues, and report unfair refusal to your LGI. NYSC gives final direction on clearance disputes.

Medical leave can affect clearance if it is not properly approved or documented. Keep medical evidence and NYSC approval.

Absconding from service is a serious breach. It can lead to extension, non-payment, disciplinary action, or failure to receive a discharge certificate.
