# NYSC Relocation and Redeployment

Relocation, also called redeployment, is the process of moving from the original state of deployment to another state through NYSC approval. It is not automatic. You must apply and provide documents that match the reason for relocation.

Commonly accepted grounds include health, marital grounds for married female corps members, security, and official NYSC directive. Do not rely on unofficial promises or payment to get relocation.

## How to Apply

In camp, interested corps members may be asked to fill a relocation form and submit supporting documents. After camp, applications are usually made from the NYSC portal dashboard under relocation or redeployment. Choose the reason, upload required documents, submit, and wait for NYSC review.

Approval time is not fixed. Some in-camp applications are decided before camp closes, while online applications can take weeks or longer. NYSC can approve, reject, or ask for more information.

## Medical Relocation

Medical relocation requires a credible medical report from a government or military hospital. The report should carry the hospital letterhead, diagnosis, doctor's name, stamp, and signature. Recent tests or imaging may be required depending on the condition. Do not fabricate medical documents.

## Marital Relocation

Marital relocation is normally for married female corps members who want to serve near their husband. Documents may include marriage certificate, affidavit where required, newspaper publication for name change, spouse's valid ID, proof of spouse's residence, and employer letter or other domicile evidence.

## Security Relocation

For security-related relocation, follow NYSC instructions for the affected state or local government area. If your PPA or community is unsafe, report quickly to your Local Government Inspector, Zonal Inspector, state secretariat, or camp officials.

## After Approval

After approval, download and print your relocation letter from the portal. Report to the NYSC state secretariat in the new state, update your records, and follow instructions for new posting or documentation. Continue serving in your original state until approval is confirmed.

Cancellation after approval is not guaranteed. Contact NYSC through official channels if you need to reverse or change an approved relocation.
