# NYSC Safety and Scam Prevention

Corps members and prospective corps members should avoid anyone promising to influence posting, relocation, allowance, camp registration, or certificate processing for a fee. NYSC processes should be handled through the official portal, camp officials, LGI, state secretariat, or institution.

Do not send portal login details, biometric data, bank details, or scanned certificates to unknown people online. Do not pay social media agents who claim they can change your state of deployment or guarantee relocation.

Check links carefully. The NYSC portal and NYSC official website are the main channels to confirm important instructions. Fake pages may copy NYSC branding.

If your PPA, lodge, route, or community feels unsafe, inform your LGI, employer, state secretariat, or security authorities quickly. Keep family or trusted colleagues aware of your location when travelling.

For medical, security, disciplinary, certificate, or legal issues, confirm with official NYSC channels. Do not rely on rumours or unofficial WhatsApp broadcasts.
