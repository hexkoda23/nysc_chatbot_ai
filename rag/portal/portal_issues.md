# NYSC Portal Issues

The NYSC portal is used for registration, dashboard access, call-up letter printing, relocation applications, correction requests, and other service actions. If the portal is not opening, check your internet connection, try another browser, clear cache, avoid peak hours, and confirm that the official portal is active.

To reset your portal password, use the password recovery option on the NYSC portal and follow the instructions sent to your registered email or phone where available.

If your dashboard shows wrong details, do not create a new account. Use the correction option shown on the portal where available, contact your institution for school-generated errors, or reach NYSC support.

Passport photograph changes after submission may be restricted. If you uploaded the wrong photo, check whether the portal provides a correction option or contact NYSC support quickly.

To check posting online, log in to your NYSC dashboard when deployment is released and print or download your call-up letter.

If biometric capture fails during registration, try a supported browser and device, clean the scanner or camera setup, and use an accredited registration point if needed. If biometric clearance fails during service, report to the LGI or clearance officer immediately.

NYSC registration dates open and close by batch and stream. Confirm closure from the NYSC portal, official NYSC announcements, your institution, or state secretariat. Do not rely only on social media posts.

After final submission, some registration details may not be editable. Review carefully before submitting.
