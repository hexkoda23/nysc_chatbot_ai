# General NYSC FAQ

Always use official NYSC channels for critical matters. The NYSC portal is used for registration and dashboard actions, while the NYSC website and state secretariat provide official announcements and guidance.

For questions involving money, relocation, medical documents, discipline, safety, certificate errors, portal failure, or final clearance, confirm from NYSC before acting. Local documents are useful for guidance, but batch-specific instructions can change.
