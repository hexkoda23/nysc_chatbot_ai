# NYSC Call-up Letter

The NYSC call-up letter is the document that tells a prospective corps member the state of deployment, orientation camp address, reporting date, call-up number, batch and stream, and important camp instructions.

You print the call-up letter from your NYSC portal dashboard after deployment is released. Log in with your registered email and password, open the relevant call-up letter option, download or print it, and keep copies for camp registration.

If you cannot access your call-up letter, check that registration and payment were completed, confirm that your dashboard is active, try again later if the portal is busy, and contact NYSC support or your school if the issue persists.

## Camp Entry

Do not go to camp without your call-up letter. It is one of the key documents checked during physical registration. Colour printing is commonly required in local NYSC guidance, and the document should not be laminated because officials may need to inspect or stamp it.

The reporting date tells you when NYSC expects you to arrive at the orientation camp. If you miss the date, report as soon as possible and follow camp officials' instructions. Late reporting can affect registration, so do not intentionally delay.

## Deployment Changes

NYSC can change deployment or correct records through official channels, but a corps member should not assume a deployment state can be changed just because the call-up letter is out. Use official NYSC procedures for relocation or correction. Do not pay anyone online to influence posting or relocation.

If your call-up number or personal details look wrong, report the issue through the portal, your institution, or NYSC support before relying on the document.
