# Community Development Service

Community Development Service, or CDS, is the NYSC programme where corps members contribute to community development outside their primary assignment. CDS is a core part of service and attendance can affect clearance.

Corps members are usually assigned to CDS groups by NYSC at the local government level. You may request a change of CDS group, but approval depends on the LGI or relevant NYSC officer and local rules.

## Attendance

CDS meetings are compulsory unless NYSC grants permission or there is a valid reason such as documented illness. Missing CDS without approval can affect monthly clearance, allowance, and final service record.

Your PPA should not stop you from attending official CDS activities. If there is a conflict, inform your LGI so the issue can be handled properly.

## Personal CDS Project

A corps member can propose a personal CDS project. The project should meet a real community need, be approved by NYSC before execution, and be documented properly. Good CDS projects can be recognized or awarded by NYSC, but awards are not automatic.

CDS attendance is one of the items NYSC may consider during clearance. If you miss CDS for medical reasons, keep evidence and report through the proper channel.
